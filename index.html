How we work

A Florida company, privately held

OIA LLC is a limited liability company registered in the State of Florida, with registration details on public record with the Florida Division of Corporations. It is privately held and holds interests in affiliated businesses.

It is not an accountancy practice, a law firm or an investment adviser, sells nothing to the public and offers no security or investment to anyone. Tax, legal and regulatory work is carried out by licensed professionals the group engages, and nothing on this site is advice of any kind.

Frequently asked

The questions we get most

What does OIA LLC actually do?

It holds interests in a group of affiliated businesses and runs their shared administration, planning and reporting. It doesn't sell services to the public.

Can I buy services from you?

No. The administrative work described here is provided to companies within the group only.

Can I invest in the group?

No. The company is privately held and makes no offer of any security or investment to anyone.

Who do I contact about a specific business?

Tell us which one and we'll route it. Each operating business has its own site and its own point of contact.

Do you give tax or legal advice?

No, to anyone. Licensed professionals are engaged for that, and nothing here is advice.

Are you hiring suppliers?

We work with trades, suppliers and advisers across the group. Get in touch and we'll tell you honestly whether there's a fit.

Looking for the right part of the group?

Tell us what it concerns and we'll point you to the right business within one working day.
