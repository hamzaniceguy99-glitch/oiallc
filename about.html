Why five companies need one back office

Each one generates the same paperwork. Doing it five times is how it stops getting done at all.

How we work

One calendar, one close, one view

Administration fails quietly in small companies. Nobody notices a renewal date until the policy has lapsed, and nobody reconstructs a year of records without losing something along the way.

Running it centrally fixes the economics. One calendar covering every filing and renewal, one filing structure, and a monthly close on a fixed date for each business.

The owners then get a consolidated view rather than five separate impressions, which is the difference between managing a group and simply owning several things at once.
